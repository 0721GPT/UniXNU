## State

The tablet reaches the iPadOS desktop with CPU rendering on eight CPUs. UFS and a 96 GiB persistent Data volume work. FT8203 touch supports taps and swipes.

The SM5714 battery gauge reports charge, voltage and status; charger hotplug interrupts are verified. USB CDC provides logs and stack capture. GPU, wireless, audio and suspend/resume remain pending.
